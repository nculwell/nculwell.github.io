When you start ACS, you are presented with the main menu:

PRESS F1 TO MAKE AN ADVENTURE DISK
PRESS F3 TO COPY YOUR ADVENTURE DISK
PRESS F5 TO CONSTRUCT AN ADVENTURE
PRESS F7 TO PLAY AN ADVENTURE

If you choose F1, it asks you to pick which adventure to create:

Rivers of Light
Land of Aventuria
Fantasy Builder Set
Spy/Mystery Builder Set
Science Fiction Builder Set

After you make a choice, it asks you to insert a blank disk to save your adventure on. You then need to swap the adventure disk and side 2 of the game disk many times as it creates the adventure disk.
